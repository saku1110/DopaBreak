# Screen-Time Competitor Feature Scan

**Generated**: 2026-07-11
**Scope**: one sec, Opal, ScreenZen, Jomo, Roots

## Recommended gaps for DopaBreak

1. Carry the user's intention through the timed session with a Live Activity and a one-tap “finish now” action.
2. Launch the user's “Today Focus” or chosen replacement app directly from the intervention.
3. Offer adaptive friction that increases gently after repeated opens, without streaks or punishment.
4. Add a one-tap “focus now” block using three opinionated durations instead of a complex rule builder.
5. Join pre-use intent and post-use feeling into one insight, such as “When you opened from boredom, it felt worthwhile 18% of the time.”
6. Consider an optional delayed intervention for communication-heavy apps: allow 1–3 minutes first, then intervene before scrolling expands.

## Explicitly avoid

- Streaks, leaderboards, coins, rewards and social competition.
- Habit completion as currency for app time.
- A general-purpose rule builder with many overlapping conditions.
- Hardware dependence or manual Shortcuts setup.
